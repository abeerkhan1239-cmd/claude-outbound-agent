An outbound agent that does the research and leaves the sending to a person.

Built on Claude Code for Adcelerate Media, a growth agency for clinics and supplement brands. It finds the decision maker, audits their website, writes a first message specific enough to get a reply, and saves it as a draft. A person sends it.

Source code How it works

The problem

Good cold outreach is slow. Generic outreach is ignored. Each lead needs three things done by hand before a message is worth sending.

01

Find the right person

Clinics and small brands rarely list who makes marketing decisions. Owner, medical director or founder has to be found and verified.

02

Look at their funnel

How do they take bookings? Are they tracking ads? Do they run any? The first line of a good message comes from here.

03

Write it in one voice

Messages go out from the co-founder, a former physician. They must sound like her and never overclaim.

How it works

One Claude Code skill runs the pipeline. Small Node scripts do the parts that must be deterministic. Gmail and Google Sheets connectors carry the output.

  1. Read new leadsRows with status = new from the Leads Google Sheet.
  2. QualifyChecked against the ICP: dental, med spa and functional-medicine clinics, and Shopify health brands. Misfits are marked skip with a reason.
  3. Find the decision makerContactOut Decision Makers API by domain, then a work email revealed for exactly one person. Only Verified addresses are used.
  4. Audit the funnelBooking vendor, tracking pixels, visible offers and site platform, plus live ads in the Meta Ad Library for brands.
  5. Draft three messagesLinkedIn connect note, follow-up DM and email, each hooked on one finding.
  6. Guardrail checklint-copy.mjs rejects drafts that break a rule. The agent rewrites until they pass.
  7. Save as draftsEmail to Gmail drafts, LinkedIn texts into the sheet. Status moves to drafted.
  8. A person reviews and sendsNothing goes out automatically. LinkedIn sends stay manual to protect the sender's profile.
  9. Triage repliesReplies are labelled interested, not now, wrong person or unsubscribe. Interested prospects get a drafted answer with the booking link.

The main safety rule

The audit tags every finding as measured (seen in the page) or unmeasured (an absence in static HTML). Site builders inject scripts at runtime, so absences can be wrong. Only measured findings may appear in a message.

Allowed: measured"Saw you book through Boulevard. Do most new patients come from Instagram or Google?"
Blocked: unmeasured"Your site doesn't have a Meta Pixel, so you're not tracking your ads."

Guardrails in code, not just in the prompt

Copy linter

Blocks em dashes, guarantees and "10x" claims, filler phrases, claims that something is missing, inaccurate credentials ("former physician", never a current title), and drafts over each channel's length limit.

Proof whitelist

The agent can only cite results listed in config/proof.md. An empty file means no results claims at all.

Credit discipline

Decision makers are listed without revealing contacts. Email credits are spent on one chosen person per company.

People own the send

The agent never sends mail and never marks a lead as sent. Only a person can move a row to sent.

Status and how impact is measured

Being rolled out on live leads. Time saved is logged rather than estimated: timelog.mjs records minutes per lead by hand (baseline) and with the agent, then reports hours saved per week. The result will be published here once a full week of data exists.
