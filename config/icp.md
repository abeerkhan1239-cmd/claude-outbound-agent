# Who we contact

One positioning: **health and wellness growth**. Two segments.

## 1. Clinics (`segment: clinic`)

| Priority | Type | Decision maker titles |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dental practices (private, 1 to 5 locations) | Owner, Practice Owner, Dentist & Owner, Practice Manager |
| 2 | Med spas / aesthetics | Owner, Medical Director, Founder, Nurse Injector & Owner |
| 3 | Functional medicine, IV, longevity | Founder, Medical Director |

Skip: DSO-owned groups (marketing is central), hospital clinics, practices with
no website.

## 2. Health and supplement brands (`segment: brand`)

Shopify brands selling supplements, vitamins, functional food or wellness products.
Decision makers: Founder, Co-Founder, CEO, Head of Growth, Head of Marketing.

Good signs: running Meta ads already (the Ad Library shows active ads), Klaviyo
installed, subscribe & save offer. Skip: Amazon-only sellers, brands with a
full in-house growth team (5+ marketing staff on LinkedIn).

## Not an ICP

Fitness coaches. Small budgets and they live on Instagram. Treat them as a
referral channel, not a target. If one appears in the sheet, mark it `skip`.

## Choosing the one person to reveal

`decision-makers` returns several people. Reveal an email for exactly one:
owner or founder first, then medical director, then marketing lead. Never more
than one per company per run; credits are finite.
